Tuna Mayo Sandwich Filling

I can't lie, gang, this one is kind of a (forgive me) filler recipe. But! It is yummy and reminds me of my final year of university when this was part of my lunch pretty much everyday.

Ingredients

  • 1 tin tuna
  • some mayonnaise? Start with 2tbsp and see how you go
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • black pepper

Method

  1. Drain the tuna and whack it in a bowl with the mayo and half the lemon juice.
  2. Mix it up and taste it. Add more mayo if there's not enough to engulf the tuna. Add more juice and pepper if you want.
  3. Use for sandwiches or as a pasta topping or mixed with whatever salad stuff you have in your fridge or whatever! Enjoy!
